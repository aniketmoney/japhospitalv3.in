Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Patients Commonly Ask

General information only. Your surgeon will advise based on your diagnosis, health and treatment plan.

When should I consider knee replacement?

Knee replacement may be discussed when pain, stiffness or loss of function remains significant despite appropriate non-surgical treatment. A clinical evaluation is needed before deciding.

What is robotic knee replacement?

Robotic-assisted knee replacement uses technology to support planning, alignment assessment and surgical execution while the surgeon remains in control of the procedure.

Is robotic knee replacement better than conventional surgery?

The most suitable approach depends on the patient, diagnosis and surgeon's assessment. Robotic assistance can provide additional planning and alignment information, but it does not replace surgical judgement.

How long will I stay in hospital?

Hospital stay varies depending on the procedure, medical condition, mobility and recovery progress. Your care team will discuss the expected stay during treatment planning.

When can I walk after knee replacement?

Mobilisation is planned individually and commonly begins under supervision as part of postoperative care and physiotherapy. Your surgeon and physiotherapy team will guide the timeline.

What is the approximate cost of knee replacement?

Cost depends on the procedure, implant, room category, investigations and individual medical requirements. Contact JAP Hospital for a personalised estimate.

Does JAP Hospital offer ECHS treatment?

JAP Hospital is ECHS empanelled. Eligibility, referral, authorisation and documentation requirements apply.
