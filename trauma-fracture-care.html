Treatment Guide

Prompt Orthopaedic Care for Fractures & Injuries

Fractures, dislocations and significant musculoskeletal injuries require timely assessment and an individual treatment plan.

Book ConsultationAll Orthopaedic Services
Specialist-led orthopaedic care: treatment planning is led by Dr. Aman Singh and the JAP Hospital orthopaedic team.
Patient Information

What to Know Before You Decide

Initial Assessment

Pain, swelling, deformity, wound status, circulation and nerve function are assessed.

Imaging

X-rays and other imaging may be used depending on the injury.

Treatment Options

May include splints, casts, reduction, fixation or surgery depending on fracture pattern and patient factors.

Follow-Up

Bone healing, movement and rehabilitation are monitored through follow-up visits.

This page provides general patient education. Diagnosis, suitability, treatment technique, hospital stay and recovery must be individualised after clinical assessment.
