For Patients From Outside Jalandhar

Travelling to JAP Hospital for Treatment?

A practical guide for Punjab, outstation and NRI families planning an orthopaedic consultation or surgery in Bhogpur.

How to Reach

JAP Hospital is located on G.T. Road / Jalandhar–Pathankot Highway in Dalli, Bhogpur, Punjab.

Before You Travel

Keep recent scans, X-rays, blood reports, medication lists and previous medical records ready for review.

Hospital Stay

Expected stay depends on the procedure, medical condition and recovery. Confirm the individual plan with the hospital team.

Attendant Planning

Ask the hospital what assistance, room arrangements and mobility support may be needed for your specific treatment.

Follow-Up

Discuss follow-up timing, wound review, physiotherapy and what can be coordinated remotely after discharge.

Accommodation & Travel

Contact the hospital before travel for current guidance about nearby accommodation and local transport options.

Planning Your Visit From Outside Bhogpur?

Message the hospital before travelling so the team can guide you on records, timing and preparation.
